      Rules and numbers in this test

      Turns

      Each unit's wait fills by SPD minus gear weight; at 100 it acts. Move and act in either order. Doing both costs 100 wait, one costs 80, neither costs 60. Pick a facing at the end.

      Hit chance

      HIT − (target SPD − 10) × 2, +10 from the flank, +20 from behind, kept between 0% and 100%.

      Damage

      ATK × power × 100 ÷ (100 + DEF), or ATN against RES for elemental abilities. Crit chance is LUK ÷ 2 (+25 from Hidden); a crit doubles damage.

      Counters

      75% front, 50% flank, 25% back. −10% per level the attacker stands above. Braced +25%. A crit halves it. Hidden attackers, Suppressed or Stunned defenders, and attackers outside the defender's weapon reach draw no counter. Counters deal 50% (75% when Braced) and always land.

      Resources

      • Mercenary line: cooldowns
      • Gunner: Charge, starts full, never refills
      • Elementalist: Focus, starts at 0, +20 a turn
      • Bloodcaller: Resonance, starts at 50, +6 a turn; when short, pays the rest in HP for +25% damage and a Strained stack (+10% damage each; 5 stacks means Burnout for 2 turns)

      Hidden

      A Hidden unit can't be targeted directly, by attacks, single-target abilities or turrets, unless it is spotted: inside an enemy's vision, which covers 2 tiles ahead, the front diagonals and 2 tiles to each side, but nothing behind. It works both ways: walking into a cone or an enemy turning to face you both spot you. Hover a unit to see its vision. A spotted unit keeps Hidden's advantages. Area abilities (Suppressing Fire, Flame Burst) always hit it, and a hit reveals it. Hidden attackers draw no counter and get +25 crit. Attacking ends Hidden.

      Not in this test

      Revives, items, towns, class changes and exact final numbers. Fallen units show a 3-turn countdown (Normal difficulty) but can't be saved yet. Area abilities only hit enemies.
